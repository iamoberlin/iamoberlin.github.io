Pass 2 — 01Resolved's analytical machinery

Distilled from their 12 mechanics/education posts (Dec 2025 – Jun 2026). This defines the computational surface a lead engineer would own.

Decision-market microstructure

  • Phases: trading period (~72h) = Pre-TWAP (~24h, "belief formation") + TWAP window (~48h, "belief enforcement"). Only TWAP-window prices count toward execution. TWAP math dilutes sniping: a trade in the last 10 min of a 48h window ≈ 0.35% of window weight.
  • Thresholds are configurable per proposal — observed range −3% to +4.1% (negative threshold = passes unless fail beats pass by that margin).
  • Economic alignment: pass-buy & fail-sell = pass-aligned; pass-sell & fail-buy = fail-aligned. Net Pass Volume Tilt = [(PassBuy + FailSell) − (PassSell + FailBuy)] / total volume; same on trade counts for Trader Tilt.
  • Late-activity taxonomy: organic close-out / activist pressure / outcome steering — classified by directional symmetry and capital in the final hours.
  • Signal-quality framework: price resilience (slippage, depth-to-1%-move, single-trade fragility) + participation quality + robustness (snap-back) + information environment. Their standing rebuttal to "low volume = invalid": low volume can mean fast consensus (Solomon DP-00002 cleared with ~4 independent trades after stripping an arb bot).
  • Conviction over time: net pass-aligned dollars accumulated, split pre-TWAP vs TWAP.

Wallet/trader forensics

  • Cross-proposal and cross-DAO wallet tracking; per-wallet ending pass/fail exposure.
  • Bot detection via cadence, paired pass-sell/fail-sell execution, ~$100 clustered sizing, near-neutral net position — then "strip out the bot" independent-trade counts.
  • Trade-frequency classification: 1–2 trades = conviction voter; 3–10 = high engagement; 21+ = arb/HFT bot. Repeat-trader (org-level retention) metrics.
  • Long-term-holder identification: linking decision-market wallets back to ICO contribution history (Ranger liquidation's biggest pass-aligned wallets were ICO-era backers who lost trust).
  • Named internal tool: "01Resolved Decision Market Analysis Model."

ICO analytics

  • Full participation datasets: committed vs accepted, oversubscription multiples, power-law cohorts (Ranger: top 50 wallets = 42.7% of $86.4M committed), contribution brackets, repeat-participant cohorting across ICOs (29% repeat wallets supplied 47% of Ranger value at >2× average ticket).
  • External-incentive vectors flagged (e.g., Polymarket cross-market hedging around ICOs).

Treasury / NAV analytics

  • Total NAV = non-native treasury assets − liabilities; NAV/AS = Total NAV ÷ Adjusted Supply (total − protocol-controlled − locked − unvested). Premium/Discount = (spot ÷ NAV/AS) − 1, with historical bands.
  • Runway as a range (with/without protocol-owned liquidity — spending POL thins the AMM and triggers reflexive selling).
  • Burn vs dilution decomposition (spend lowers both NAV lines; supply growth lowers only NAV/AS) + a 4-row NAV/price signal matrix.
  • Projected NAV from program-enforced schedules: monthly allowances, performance packages (cliffs + price milestones), approved raises, revenue forecasts.
  • Buyback evaluation: size as % of non-LP treasury, utilization, post-buyback runway range, performance vs SOL benchmark. Key finding: buybacks must be sized to what the treasury can absorb (Loyal spent 79.8% of treasury; Solomon's 17.1% aged far better).

Governance/mechanism R&D (they do design work, not just analytics)

  • Permissionless-proposals RFC (Mar 2026): split proposal creation (permissionless) from market activation (stake threshold + published per-DAO "proposal constitution"). Guardrail menu: Sensitive vs Standard proposal classes, onchain runway-floor checks, class-tuned thresholds/windows, stake calibration (~5–10% liquid supply standard, 15–20% sensitive). Roadmap included a proposal-creation wizard and possible fork of the MetaDAO decision-market program as reference implementation. They offered consulting to DAOs on guardrail selection and were prepared to submit a formal MetaDAO proposal.
  • Case studies showing futarchy as negotiation forum: Solomon "Gigabus" ($2.7M volume, 2,276 trades) was failing until the team negotiated mid-TWAP with the lead opposing whale (adding Proph3t as multisig signer, returning ~$2M) and the market repriced.

MetaDAO mechanism facts (for reference)

  • Proposals need stake backing to go live; post-ICO 90-day restriction on buybacks/liquidation (nullifiable by proposal); program-enforced monthly team allowances; FutarchyAMM shares liquidity between pass/fail conditional pools; losing branch reverts; ICOs = minimum raise + capped acceptance with pro-rata oversubscription.

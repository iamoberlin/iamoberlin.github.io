Market — MetaDAO, futarchy, ownership coins

Formulas and microstructure live in 03-methods.md. This note is the landscape 01Resolved is levered to.

Mechanism (short)

Futarchy: paired conditional markets price the org if a proposal passes vs fails. It executes only if pass TWAP beats fail TWAP by a threshold in the TWAP window. Trades are classified by economic exposure (pass-buy and fail-sell are pass-aligned), not surface direction.

Ownership coins (MetaDAO ICO framework, first launch Apr 2025 — MtnDAO): high-float fair issuance, proceeds in an onchain treasury under decision-market control, program-enforced monthly spend, IP in a DAO-linked entity, team tokens behind an 18-month cliff + performance milestones.

Ownership Score (20–21 Jul 2026): daily, wallet score += USD holdings with 1%/day decay; META weighted 25%; retroactive to 115K+ wallets. Up to 50% of Rip Cars was allocated by score. Flagship findings: 170 wallets (0.15%) hold 50% of score; score-allocated Rip Cars wallets kept 63% of tokens on day 1 vs 18% for others. Crude, but better than allocating without it. Individual scores were publicly disputed; MetaDAO says the formula may change.

MetaDAO

  • Launchpad live 9 Apr 2025. Founders: Proph3t and Kollan House. SF, ~1–10 people. ~$12.1M raised (Paradigm $2.2M in 2024 + $9.9M OTC Oct 2025 from Paradigm/Variant/6MV). Pantera is not an investor — its Feb 2024 $50K META buy was rejected by the market.
  • Revenue: FutarchyAMM 0.5% (fully to MetaDAO since Dec 2025) + Meteora LP. 01Resolved measured ~$127K (Jun) → ~$245K (Jul), +93% MoM. Job listings have claimed ~$9M/yr; treat that as unverified.
  • Template: 200K–1.5M token stake; one live proposal per DAO; 3-day markets; lagging TWAP with 24h pre-record; thresholds −3% team / +3% non-team. “MetaDAO 2.0” is community shorthand (proposal-type thresholds, NAV-guided buys/sells, elected committee), not a named MetaDAO program.
  • Traction: 2025 projects raised $96M combined; Umbra ICO $154M committed; Rip Cars 128×; ownership-coin mcap $159M (9 Aug). META on Coinbase.

Stress-tests they covered

  • Umbra (Aug 2026) — fresh wallet, deBridge from Base, exact 500K stake, $1.5M (~62% of treasury) request. Failed 19.55% below threshold; attacker cost $4,623. Pools ~$52K. This repo’s scorer is that pattern.
  • Ranger — market liquidated the treasury.
  • ZKFG/Zinc — revenue kept off-treasury → buyout, DAO dissolved.
  • Flash Trade — winding down unless acquired.
  • Thin-market governance: a $2M MetaDAO allocation passed on ~$3K volume.

Deaths so far: mtnCapital, Ranger, ZKFG, Flash Trade, plus FairScale on Combinator.

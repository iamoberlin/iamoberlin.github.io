MetaDAO: the platform 01Resolved is levered to

  • Origins/funding: DAO launched Nov 2023 with a $10K treasury; launchpad live Apr 9, 2025. Funding ≈ $12.1M: $2.2M led by Paradigm (Aug 2024) + $9.9M OTC (Oct 2025: Paradigm $5.9M, Variant $2.5M, 6MV $1.5M). Pantera is not an investor — its Feb 2024 proposal to buy $50K of META was rejected by the decision market (a famous futarchy moment).
  • Founders: pseudonymous Proph3t (@metaproph3t) and Kollan House ("Nallok" — note: "Kollan House" appears as a tracked project slug too). Team ~1–10 people, SF. Hiring via third-party boards: Founding Engineer $150–250K, Web3 Staff Engineer $250–400K listings seen; their Ashby board currently shows 0 published postings.
  • Revenue: FutarchyAMM 0.5% fee (fully to MetaDAO since Dec 2025) + Meteora LP; ~$2.4M cumulative by early 2026; job listings claim ~$9M/yr run rate; 01Resolved measured ~$245K in July (+93% MoM). Blockworks Analytics runs live MetaDAO revenue dashboards.
  • Mechanics (docs.metadao.fi): proposals need 200K–1.5M tokens staked (1–15% of the 10M ICO float); one live proposal at a time per DAO; no slashing. Markets trade 3 days seeded with half the project's spot liquidity; "lagging price TWAP" with 24h pre-recording delay; thresholds −3% team-sponsored / +3% non-team. Launch template: 10M tokens to sale, 2.9M liquidity, ≤12.9M performance package unlocking in 5 tranches at 2×/4×/8×/16×/32× ICO price (3-month TWAP, ≥18 months); monthly team budget ≤ 1/6 of min raise; IP to a Cayman SPC. New: Colosseum STAMP (pre-ICO instrument; investor reserve ≤20%, team 10–40%, 24-month linear unlock).
  • "MetaDAO 2.0" is 01Resolved's/community shorthand from the July monthly (proposal- type thresholds, programmatic NAV-based buys/sells, elected oversight committee) — not a formally named MetaDAO initiative. META migration: METAC → META 1:1000; listed on Coinbase, KuCon, Upbit (as META2), Bithumb.
  • Traction: first 8 ICOs $25.6M; 2025 projects raised $96M combined; Umbra ICO drew $154M in commitments; Rip Cars 128×; ownership-coin market cap $159M (Aug 9).

Adjacent launch venues (their expansion surface)

  • Futardio (futard.io) — MetaDAO's permissionless arm. Live counters: $43.7M committed, 2,210 funders. High failure rate (Mar: 52 launches, 2 hit target).
  • Star (star.fun) — "internet rounds": tokenized startup equity on bonding curves; raised $450K for itself in 48h; ~$1.57M raised on platform.
  • Spark — IdeaCoins: backers fund ideas, builders compete, decision markets govern; integrated Omnipair for instant liquidity; Jurassic $RAWR ($12.5M committed).
  • Others: Crafts (SAFE-in-SPV, Arcium sealed bids), Fair.Club, Street, Bedrock (equity-via-token), Combinator (FairScale liquidation happened there). Hurupay = a fintech that ICO'd on MetaDAO (not a venue).

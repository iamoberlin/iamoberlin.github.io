Risks & open questions to probe

Risks

  1. Single-ecosystem dependency. The business is levered to MetaDAO's success, and MetaDAO has said mechanisms (e.g., Ownership Score formula) may change under them at any time. What does "beyond MetaDAO" concretely mean, and on what timeline?
  2. Market size today. $5.86K of ecosystem-wide decision-market volume in July 2026 and a 103-subscriber Telegram. The entire market could stay niche; the bet only pays if ownership coins keep compounding (93% MoM MetaDAO revenue growth is the counter-signal).
  3. Unproven revenue model. No visible pricing, no announced funding, founders publish under first names only. Verified Aug 12: no funding trace anywhere — not in press, Crunchbase/CB Insights, their own 43 posts, or X citations; they have not ICO'd on MetaDAO/Futardio themselves either. Presumably bootstrapped — yet someone paid for a VAR META agency build (typically low-to-mid six figures), so ask how it's financed.
  4. Neutrality vs ecosystem intimacy. Credibility depends on independence, yet MetaDAO's founder amplifies their work, they presented at MetaDAO's Owners Meeting, and API keys are granted by DM. They do disclose trading on Ride Markets themselves. How will research stay honest as commercial ties deepen?
  5. Incumbent risk. Blockworks already runs MetaDAO revenue dashboards and owns the disclosure standard (TTF + Transparency Alliance with Coinbase/Kraken/Grayscale). 01Resolved positions as complementary — but Blockworks expanding downward, or MetaDAO building analytics in-house, are both live threats. (Flip side: Blockworks is also the most plausible partner/acquirer.)
  6. Their own data quality. The July monthly restates June's headline metrics differently than the June issue did (DM volume $1.42M vs $1.39M, 200 vs 165 wallets) — even flagship numbers drift between their own reports. Supports the "correctness as moat" pitch, and worth raising tactfully.

Questions for the founders

  • Runway and funding: bootstrapped? Any outside capital? What's the burn?
  • Entity and equity: is Organization Technology LLC the employer? How is the lead engineer compensated — cash / equity / tokens? Cap table basics.
  • Current architecture: who built the indexer/classification engine, what's the debt, what breaks most often? (The /treasury-intelligence 500 is a conversation opener.)
  • Revenue plan sequencing: API tiers vs OpenBooks vs signals — which first, and who are the first three paying customers they have in mind?
  • Relationship with MetaDAO: formal partnership? Data access guarantees? Would MetaDAO build this in-house? (MetaDAO's X bio says "We're hiring!" — they're staffing up too.)
  • Team plans: is lead engineer hire #1? What's the 12-month hiring plan?
  • What happened with the disputed Ownership Scores — was the error theirs, MetaDAO's, or wallet-attribution ambiguity (same owner, multiple wallets)?

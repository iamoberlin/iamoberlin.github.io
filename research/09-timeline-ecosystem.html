Signals about the business:

  • Team is at least 3 (Drew, James, Ian SB) and growing; self-described as "founders, investors, and finance operators."
  • No pricing, revenue, funding, or entity details disclosed anywhere. OpenBooks is pitched to ownership-coin teams; Loyal is the de facto showcase (the only project among 53 Blockworks TTF filings with a balance sheet — via a linked report).
  • Their editorial "Calls" function as a product roadmap: proposal constitutions (Feb) → market-based curation (Mar) → financial legibility/OpenBooks (May) → fundamental-analysis culture (Jun) → revenue-routing standard (Jul). Two of their proposals were later echoed in production (P2P's guardrails, Star.Fun's curation markets) — they have real ecosystem influence.
  • They disclose trading on Ride Markets themselves (a conflict-of-interest disclosure norm).
  • Blockworks TTF positioning: explicitly complementary — TTF is the disclosure standard; 01Resolved is the verification/legibility layer beneath it. Their finding: of 53 TTF filings, only 2 include an income statement and only Loyal has a balance sheet. Their recurring frame: "transparency ≠ legibility."

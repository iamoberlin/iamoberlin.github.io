Company — 01Resolved

Two-founder analytics layer for ownership coins and decision markets: a Bloomberg/Messari-style platform plus a research publication for MetaDAO’s Solana futarchy ecosystem. Beta launched 27 Dec 2025.

Thesis (their words, across Crypto's Ownership Crisis and Building in the Open): tokens rarely delivered enforceable ownership; MetaDAO fixes the structure; the model only works if that transparency is legible. Raw transfers are not a P&L. 01Resolved is the missing layer — “the teams that chose transparency first should not have to do it alone.”

Company profile — 01Resolved

Compiled 2026-08-12 from public sources.

One-liner

01Resolved is a two-founder startup building the analytics/intelligence layer for ownership coins and decision markets — a Bloomberg/Messari-style platform plus a research publication for the MetaDAO (futarchy) ecosystem on Solana. Beta launched December 27, 2025.

Thesis

Laid out across "Crypto's Ownership Crisis" (Dec 2025) and "Building in the Open" (Apr 2026):

  • Most crypto tokens never delivered enforceable ownership. The token-equity split era (offshore foundation + operating company) fractured value flow; holders got "participation without ownership" (Aave/CoW revenue dispute, UNI front-end fees, holders excluded from Axelar/Tensor/Padre acquisitions).
  • MetaDAO's ownership-coin framework fixes the structure: high-float fair issuance, ICO proceeds locked in an onchain treasury governed by decision markets, IP assigned to a DAO-linked entity, founder pay behind performance milestones.
  • But the model survives only if its radical transparency is legible. Raw onchain data doesn't say whether a transfer is revenue, an expense, or an internal move. A Dune dashboard is unverified and incomparable across projects.
  • 01Resolved builds that missing layer: "the teams that chose transparency first should not have to do it alone."

Market context — MetaDAO, futarchy, ownership coins

Compiled 2026-08-12. Pass-2 additions at the bottom.

The mechanism

  • Futarchy (Robin Hanson: "vote on values, bet on beliefs"): governance proposals are decided by paired conditional markets. One market prices the org if the proposal passes, the other if it fails. The proposal executes only if the pass-market TWAP exceeds the fail-market TWAP by a configured threshold during the TWAP window.
  • Pass edge = ((passTWAP − failTWAP) / failTWAP) × 100; passes when ≥ twapThreshold.
  • Economic direction: fail-sell is pass-aligned, pass-sell is fail-aligned — trades are classified by economic exposure, not surface direction (01Resolved's framing).
  • Ownership coins (MetaDAO's ICO framework, first launch Apr 2025 — MtnDAO): high-float fair issuance, proceeds to onchain treasury under decision-market control, monthly spending limits enforced by the treasury program, IP assigned to DAO-linked entity, team tokens behind an 18-month cliff + performance milestones.
  • Ownership Score (introduced Jul 20-21, 2026): daily, wallet score += USD value of ownership-coin holdings, with 1%/day decay; META weighted 25%. Assigned retroactively to 115K+ wallets. Up to 50% of the Rip Cars raise was allocated by score.

Mechanism stress-tests (all covered by 01Resolved)

  • Umbra treasury attack (Aug 2026): fresh wallet funded ~$149K via deBridge from Base, accumulated exactly the 500K UMBRA proposal stake, proposed moving $1.5M (~62% of treasury). Market rejected it 19.55% below threshold; attacker lost only $4,623. Defense was 4 distributed wallets ($165K net opposition). Conditional pools held just $52K — thin depth is the exposure. Their proposed fixes: stake locked for proposal duration, thresholds scaling with treasury share requested, deeper pools per proposal type.
  • Ranger Finance: decision market liquidated the project's treasury (early 2026).
  • ZKFG/Zinc: revenue kept outside the DAO treasury → trust dispute → structured buyout of IP, DAO dissolved. Basis of their "route 100% of revenue through the treasury" position.
  • Flash Trade: winding down unless acquired (Aug 2026).
  • Ownership Score correctness: individual scores publicly disputed on X after the snapshot; MetaDAO says the formula may change; retroactivity rewarded conviction but top scorers mostly didn't participate in the raise it gated.

Ownership Score / Rip Cars key findings (their flagship analysis)

  • 170 wallets (0.15%) hold 50% of all score; top 1,000 hold 78.8%.
  • 70% of Rip Cars contributors carried some score (77% of capital), but participants held only 7.6% of all score in existence; 3 of the 5 largest commitments had zero score.
  • Day-1 retention: score-allocated wallets kept 63% of tokens vs 18% for others; top score quartile sold 26% vs 60-75% for lower quartiles.
  • Conclusion: the score is crude but beat allocating without it; they committed to 1-week and 1-month retention re-runs.

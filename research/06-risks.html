Risks and questions

Risks

  1. Single-ecosystem dependency. Levered to MetaDAO, which can change mechanisms (Ownership Score already might). “Beyond MetaDAO” needs a date.
  2. Market size. $5.86K of ecosystem DM volume in July 2026 and 103 Telegram subscribers. Pays only if ownership coins keep compounding (+93% MoM MetaDAO revenue is the counter-signal).
  3. Unproven revenue. No pricing, no announced funding, first-name founders. No ICO of their own. Someone paid VAR META (typically low-to-mid six figures) — ask who.
  4. Neutrality vs intimacy. Proph3t amplifies them; they presented at the Owners Meeting; keys are DMs. They do disclose trading on Ride Markets.
  5. Incumbent risk. Blockworks owns TTF + MetaDAO revenue dashboards. Complementary today. Blockworks moving down, or MetaDAO building in-house, are live. Flip side: Blockworks is also the obvious partner/acquirer.
  6. Their own data quality. July monthly restates June differently than the June issue. Supports the “correctness as moat” pitch; raise it tactfully.

Questions for the founders

  • Runway, outside capital, burn. Is Organization Technology LLC the employer? Cash / equity / tokens?
  • Who built the indexer, what’s the debt, what breaks? (/treasury-intelligence 500 is an opener.)
  • Revenue sequencing: API vs OpenBooks vs Signals — first three paying logos?
  • Formal MetaDAO partnership or data-access guarantee? Their X bio says “We’re hiring!” — they are staffing too.
  • Is lead engineer hire #1? 12-month plan?
  • Ownership Score disputes: their bug, MetaDAO’s, or multi-wallet attribution?
